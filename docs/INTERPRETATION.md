# Interpretation

Final rows are cross-population-recurrent, order-distant, high-information
candidate dependencies. MI/NMI are unsigned dependence magnitudes. Enriched and
depleted drivers describe contingency architecture; they do not establish
molecular mechanism, fitness epistasis or causality. HC recurrence is a
replication filter, not a corrected p/q value. Neff measures dispersion of the
same driver across HC backgrounds. No significance/FDR claim is made by v1.
